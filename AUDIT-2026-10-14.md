## Systemic findings

1. **Snapshots presented as "today".** Three widgets show fixed prices as current values with nothing saying when
   they were taken: "what if I had bought" (a May snapshot, DOT 3.6× and ADA 2.3× too high, relative presets 5 months
   stale), the mining fallbacks ($77,300 / 136T printed in the source block beside a live $83,565 / 132.7T), and the
   Bitcoin Pizza Day quick answer. This is the class that recurs on every pass, and it needs a date shown next to
   each snapshot.
2. **2024–mid-2026 market assumptions frozen in prose.** Gas at 10 Gwei with "$2–15 swaps", a 30M block gas limit,
   L1 bridge gas of "$2–10", ETH staking "3.5–4.5% + 1–3% MEV", difficulty "~145T / 850 EH/s", BTC at $60k. Each was
   true at some point and is now off by 2× to 100×.
3. **Locale FAQs are independent editorial layers.** On mining, cashback, Polymarket and retirement the six
   languages contradicted each other (Gemini's cap was "$200 of cashback" in EN, "no cap" in PT and RU, and
   "$200/month spend" in TR; Fold's card was "from BlockFi, revived in 2026" in RU). Each language had to be fixed on
   its own numbers.
4. **22 Portuguese FAQ blocks have no diacritics at all** (visible text and FAQPage JSON-LD). 3 fixed here; the other
   19 are queued as a separate task.
