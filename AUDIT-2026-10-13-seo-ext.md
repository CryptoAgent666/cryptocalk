Third pass of the day, after `AUDIT-2026-10-13.md` and `AUDIT-2026-10-13-how-inputs.md`. **Not deployed.**

## Scope and method

- `src/i18n/calculator-seo-ext.ts` holds the eight sections rendered below "How to use" / "inputs": interpret,
  scenarios, checklist, mistakes, benchmarks, execution, hygiene, validation (2 paragraphs each). 12,288 paragraphs
  in total; only the sections enabled by `getActiveSections()` render — 10,344 paragraphs (1,488 sit in disabled
  sections and never render; not reviewed). No page falls back to the generic `seoBodyText` for these sections.
- Each active paragraph in all six languages was compared with the component (inputs, labels, units, defaults,
  presets, rendered rows, live vs typed data). In scope: what the calculator has, shows or lets you do, and figures
  explicitly attributed to it. Out of scope: general advice, market and regulatory facts, worked examples not
  attributed to the tool (several arithmetic slips there are listed in the notes, unchanged).
- Only false sentences were rewritten; replacement figures were recomputed from each component's own formula.
- **asic-mining-calculator excluded** — its copy and component were rewritten on branch
  `claude/infallible-blackburn-ce8d67` (2eac3c412). gpu-mining fixes were re-checked against that branch's
  `GpuMiningCalculator` change (fb83e69e0: data feed only; same coins and inputs).
