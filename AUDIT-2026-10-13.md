## Systemic findings (cross-page)

1. **Raw HTML shown to readers on every template page.** `LocalizedCalculatorPage.astro` rendered the
   seo-content / seo-ext paragraphs as `<p>{p}</p>`, but 1,735 of those strings carry internal links as
   `<a href="/slug/">…</a>` — so readers saw the literal markup (4 paragraphs on `/inheritance-tax-calculator/`
   alone). Present since the "contextual links" commit `9325f042d` (2026-03-10). **Fixed:** `renderProse()`
   escapes everything and restores only same-site root-relative anchors.
2. **The live mining feed is dead.** ASIC and GPU mining fetch whattomine through two CORS proxies:
   `api.codetabs.com` times out after 20 s, `corsproxy.io` answers 403 `keyless_legacy_url`. Every visitor
   sees "Failed to fetch live network data. Using fallback metrics." and May-2026 fallbacks (difficulty 145T /
   BTC $77,300 vs live 132.7T / ~$83,500). /methodology/ claimed mining yields "are fetched at page load …
   never hand-edited". **Methodology text fixed (EN/PT/TR); feed NOT fixed** — needs a server-side proxy.
3. **"How to use" / "What do the inputs mean?" describe tools that do not exist** on 9 of 20 pages
   (growth-rate and horizon fields, state-level estate tax, per-trade entry and CSV export, MEV-boost toggle,
   end-price input, buy-and-hold comparison…). Rewritten for the two inheritance calculators (6 languages);
   **the other pages are left** — this is a site-wide template class that needs its own sweep.
4. **DATA-1 at scale: the widget was fixed, the prose below it was not.** June's constant fixes reached the
   `.tsx` files but not the tables, FAQs and quick answers that quote the same figures.
